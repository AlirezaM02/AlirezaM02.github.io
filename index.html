01BareshANACAV · B2C ENERGY PRODUCT+

A utility platform designed around industrial-town customers.

I led Baresh from day one, building the early backend and data layer for a B2C electricity platform serving industrial towns. It handles packages, SMS, notifications, usage dashboards, and rankings, all sitting on top of a dynamic data source layer with its own intake SDK, so we can switch providers without touching the product. Job scheduling backs off on its own when the database is under pressure, using live pressure readings alongside each job's own run history, and every stage keeps clean data provenance so nothing gets lost between jobs. If a source falls behind, a sensor triggered service call fills the gap, and all of it is watched through Grafana.

35,000entities served with usage updates under ten minutes.Aggregate dashboards, even a month of daily totals or a day of 15 minute slots, respond in under a second.
